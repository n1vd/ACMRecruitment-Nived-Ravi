# Insight Architect - Students Performance Dataset

## Objective

I performed Exploratory Data Analysis on the Students Performance Dataset to understand patterns and relationships between different features.

## Visualizations

### 1. Math Score Histogram

Shows the distribution of math scores among students.

### 2. Average Scores by Gender

Compares the average math, reading and writing scores of male and female students.

### 3. Math Scores by Test Preparation

A box plot comparing math scores of students who completed the test preparation course and those who did not.

### 4. Reading Score vs Writing Score

A scatter plot showing the relationship between reading and writing performance.

### 5. Correlation Heatmap

Shows the correlation between math, reading and writing scores.

## Key Findings

* Most students scored within the middle to higher score range.
* Female students generally performed better in reading and writing.
* Male students generally had a higher average math score.
* Students who completed test preparation generally performed better.
* Reading and writing scores have a strong positive relationship.
* All three exam scores are positively correlated.
